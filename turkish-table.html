Fethiye · the fifteen
The market list
Calibration from the Tuesday stalls: dried figs ~₺320/kg · kükürtsüz apricots ~₺420 · pistachios ~₺1,100. Tourist multiples of these mean walk one row deeper. Skip: the bazaar side, "saffron", pre-ground spices in open sun. Olive-stall vocabulary: tatlı mild · acı bitter · yağlı oily (a compliment) · etli fleshy · yeni mahsul new crop · salamura plain brine. Two legal warnings: "salep" powder is fake (real orchid-root salep is export-banned — drink it here, don\'t buy it) and anything genuinely antique — coins, icons, old kilims — is illegal to take out of Turkey. New kilims are fine; "Ottoman" anything is not.
