        Every round is dated here, newest first. New facts get appended with the day they were checked.

          Match % is each show's five trait scores plus its fan rating, weighted by your dials. The five trait scores are an editorial read, not data from anywhere. The five green bars on each row follow the first five dials, in order.

          Fan ratings (★) are MyAnimeList user scores out of 10 for each show's first season, read on 10 Oct 2026. Scores on shows with few ratings move around more.

          Streaming and episode counts were checked on JustWatch US, Wikipedia and MyAnimeList on 10 Oct 2026. Awards listed are Crunchyroll Anime Awards unless named otherwise. Catalogs rotate, so confirm before you sit down. Hours assume about 24 minutes an episode.

          Seen marks, your queue, your own scores and dial settings are saved in this browser, and to anime-picks.json in your repo when ☁ Sync has a token. The "Open on" buttons run a title search on that service.
